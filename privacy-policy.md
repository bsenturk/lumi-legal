===========================
WHAT INFORMATION WE COLLECT
===========================

Automatically collected
-----------------------
  - Your device's Internet Protocol (IP) address
  - Screens you open in the Application, and the time and date you open them
  - Time spent in the Application
  - Your mobile device's operating system and device model

Identifiers
-----------
  - Anonymous account identifier. On first launch the Application creates an
    anonymous account through Firebase Authentication. This produces a persistent
    identifier (a "UID") that is not linked to your name, email address, or Apple
    Account. The same UID is used to identify you in our database, in our
    analytics, and in our subscription provider's records.
  - Push notification token. A device token (FCM token) used solely to deliver
    the notifications you have enabled.

Location
--------
The Application collects your device's location to determine your approximate
geographical position and uses it as follows:
  - UV data. Your coordinates are sent to Apple WeatherKit to retrieve the
    real-time UV index for where you are.
  - Notifications. Your most recent coordinates are stored on our servers so
    that scheduled notifications (for example a morning UV briefing or a high-UV
    alert) can reference the UV conditions at your location.
  - Analytics. The name of the city derived from your coordinates is recorded in
    our analytics alongside the UV reading.

Your skin type
--------------
Your selected skin profile (Fair, Medium, or Dark) is used to calculate how long
your sunscreen protection lasts and to personalise the wording of your reminders.

Your skin profile is recorded in our analytics, both as an event parameter and as
a persistent user property attached to your anonymous UID. We use it to understand
how different users apply sunscreen and to improve the accuracy of our reapply
intervals.

Subscription status
-------------------
Whether you hold an active Lumi Pro subscription is stored on our servers and with
our subscription provider. We do not receive or store your payment card details —
all payments are handled by Apple.

Settings we store on our servers
--------------------------------
  - Time zone
  - System language preference
  - Which notification types you have enabled

Stored only on your device
--------------------------
Your sunscreen application history — the time of each application, the UV index
captured at that moment, your skin profile at that moment, and the protection
interval — is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.
Uninstalling the Application permanently deletes this history.

=================================================
  - Show you the UV index for your location

  - Diagnose crashes and errors

      Legal basis (GDPR Art. 6): Our legitimate interest in keeping the

You can withdraw consent at any time by turning off the relevant permission in the
iOS Settings app. Withdrawing location permission stops UV retrieval; withdrawing
notification permission stops all reminders.

==================
DELETING YOUR DATA
==================
Uninstalling the Application stops all further collection, deletes your locally
stored sunscreen history, and ends analytics collection from your device.

Uninstalling does not automatically delete the record already held in our database.
Our backend exists only to send the notifications you enabled; it does not run an
automated deletion process. To have your stored data erased, email
buraksenturktr@icloud.com and we will delete your record.
